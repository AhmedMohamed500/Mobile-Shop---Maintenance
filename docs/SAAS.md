# SaaS model

A tenant is one independent repair business. Branches, users, roles, customers, brands, faults, repairs, messages, and audit records are tenant-owned. Branch assignment further narrows ordinary employee visibility.

Plans hold monthly/yearly pricing and JSON limits. Subscriptions support trial, active, past due, suspended, expired, and cancelled states without coupling to a payment provider. The initial seed assigns a manual trial plan. Enforcement middleware and platform administration are scheduled for the third target.

Platform support access must become a separate, time-bound, explicitly audited flow; platform operators should not reuse tenant employee authentication.
